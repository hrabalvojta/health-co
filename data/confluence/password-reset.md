# Password Reset Guide — EuroHealth Insurance AG

## Self-Service Password Reset

### Step-by-Step
1. Navigate to https://password.eurohealth.internal
2. Enter your email address (firstname.lastname@eurohealth.eu)
3. Select authentication method:
   - SMS code to registered phone
   - Authenticator app (Microsoft Authenticator recommended)
4. Enter the verification code
5. Create a new password following the requirements below
6. Password change takes effect immediately for most services

### Password Requirements
- Minimum 12 characters
- At least 1 uppercase letter
- At least 1 lowercase letter
- At least 1 number
- At least 1 special character (!@#$%^&*)
- Cannot reuse last 10 passwords
- Passwords expire every 90 days

## If Self-Service Is Not Working
1. Try using a different browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox)
2. Clear your browser cache and cookies
3. If still not working, contact IT Support:
   - Email: it-support@eurohealth.eu
   - Phone: +49 (0) 69 000 000 (internal: 5555)
   - Teams: IT Support channel
   - Hours: Mon–Fri, 08:00–18:00 CET

## Account Lockout
Your account locks after 5 failed login attempts. Wait 30 minutes for automatic unlock, or contact IT Support for immediate unlock.

## Services Affected
Password change applies to: Email (Outlook), VPN, Intranet, ServiceNow, SAP, and all SSO-integrated applications.
